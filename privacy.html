Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Learn Socratian respects your privacy. This policy explains, in general terms, how information may be handled when you use the Learn Socratian website and its educational services.

Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, Learn Socratian may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, account information, messages you send through the website, and information needed to provide access to purchased educational content.

How information may be used

  • To create and manage user accounts.
  • To provide access to lessons, practice materials, assessments, and purchases.
  • To respond to questions or support requests.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the website and its services.
  • To meet applicable legal and administrative requirements.

Payments

Payment arrangements for Learn Socratian are still being finalized. This policy will be updated before paid services are launched to identify the payment method or provider used and the information involved in processing payments.

Embedded content

Learn Socratian may embed educational videos hosted by third-party services such as YouTube. When you interact with embedded content, the third-party service may process information according to its own privacy practices.

Cookies and similar technologies

The website may use technologies needed for functions such as account sessions, preferences, security, or embedded services. This section will be updated as those systems are finalized.

Sharing of information

Learn Socratian does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate the website, provide requested services, process payments, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

Data retention and security

Reasonable measures will be used to protect information and to retain it only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to legal and operational requirements.

Your choices

You may contact Learn Socratian through the website's Contact page with questions about your information or requests concerning your account. Specific procedures will be added as the account system is finalized.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Learn Socratian adds account, payment, analytics, or other services. The date at the top of this page will indicate the latest revision.

Contact
